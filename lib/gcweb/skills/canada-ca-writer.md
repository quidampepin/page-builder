# Canada.ca Web Content Writer (voice & tone reference)

## Writing rules

1. **Plain language first** — Flesch-Kincaid grade 6–8 where possible.
2. **Active voice** — Reserve passive voice for cases where the actor is unknown or irrelevant.
3. **Verbs over nominalizations** — Write "apply" not "make an application"; "decide" not "make a decision."
4. **No jargon or idioms** — Avoid bureaucratic language. When a term has a specific legal meaning, preserve it.
5. **Sentence case for headings** — Capitalize only the first word and proper nouns. Never Title Case.
6. **No punctuation in headings** — No periods, colons. Question marks only if the heading is a question.
7. **List capitalization** — Do NOT capitalize the first letter of list items unless the list appears directly under a heading with no intro sentence. Always add a colon at the end of the intro sentence.
8. **Accessible and inclusive** — Follow WCAG 2.0. Use gender-inclusive language.
9. **Scannable structure** — Headings, short paragraphs, bulleted lists. Inverted pyramid.
10. **"On this page" TOC** — If content has 3+ H2 headings, include one at the top linking to each H2 (H2 only, never deeper).
11. **Never invent content** — Don't fabricate facts or add information not in the source.
12. **No summarizing** — When rewriting, produce full replacement content.
